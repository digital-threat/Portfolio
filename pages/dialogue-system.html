Behind the Text Box: Flexible Dialogue System in “A Search For a Shiny Thing”

Introduction

In 2024, I co-founded a tiny indie studio called The Notlanders and for the next two years I worked on a photography adventure game titled “A Search for a Shiny Thing”. Although the project is currently on indefinite hiatus, it's development presented many interesting challenges which required creative solutions. In this article, I will walk you through the dialogue system I developed for the game.

The Problem

For “A Search For a Shiny Thing” we endeavored to create a tiny but dense world full of things to explore. The game places a large emphasis on narrative, exploration and puzzle solving. The game features many colorful characters the protagonist meets along the way. Discovering the people on the island was just as important as discovering locations. It's a coming of age story about ... We wanted the NPCs to have real conversations rather than single repeating lines of dialogue.
