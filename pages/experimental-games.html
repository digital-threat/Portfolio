Experimental Games

In my last semester at Uppsala University I took a course called

Experimental Game Design

during which I developed 4 digital games in a team with 2 other students. I was the only programmer on the team, all code is written by me. Each game was developed in 2 weeks based on a theme that was set by the teacher. I'm increadibly thankful to have had Cedric le Therisien and Oliver Linke as my teammates, who agreed to follow my ideas for the first 3 games and co-designed them with me into a coherent whole.

  • 2023
  • Uppsala University
  • Unity
  • C#
  • Team Size: 3
  • Gameplay Programmer
  • Lead Designer

Frog Choir

Year: 2023

Engine: Unity

Duration: 2 weeks

Team Size: 3

Role: Programmer, designer

Frog choir is made in the style of Game & Watch. You play as a choir conductor that must keep the frog choir from going off pitch. The better you are able to keep the frogs on pitch, the larger the audience and your high score.

Comments:

Originally, I envisioned each frog would have a different voice register (soprano, alto, etc.) as well as have different singing parts. Like a real choir! We weren�t quite able to realize that idea in reality and I still wonder how the choir would sound if we did.
